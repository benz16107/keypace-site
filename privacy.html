Privacy Policy

Keypace for macOS · Last updated 7 October 2026

Keypace is a typing-speed test for macOS. Your data stays on your Mac and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.

What we collect

Nothing. Keypace does not collect or sell any personal data, and we run no server.

  • The app has no account, no login, and no sign-up.
  • The app contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
  • Your typing results, settings, streaks, and goals are stored on your Mac. They are never sent to us.

iCloud sync

If your Mac is signed in to iCloud, Keypace syncs your test history through your private iCloud database so it follows you across your own Macs. Apple stores that copy under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy terms. We cannot read it. Your settings stay on your Mac. You can turn this off for Keypace in System Settings under iCloud.

Purchases

Keypace Pro is an optional one-time purchase. Apple processes it through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Data you can see and control

Your test history and personal stats live on your Mac. You can delete them at any time from within the app. Removing the app removes its local data.

Children's privacy

Keypace does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
