Keypace

A fast, private, native typing test for Mac.

Free. Requires macOS 26 or later.

Keypace is a clean, distraction-free typing-speed test built for macOS. Practice with words, quotes, real code, or your own text; track your WPM, accuracy, and a daily streak, stored on your Mac and synced through your own iCloud.
